# Changelog

## v2.0 — November 5, 2026

- Kept the existing light theme, green `#5cba40`, navigation, and SIH&S name.
- Removed unrelated branding and Delaware-only service limits. Coverage is confirmed per service and contract.
- Sign-in adds remember-me, a demo biometrics button, guest requests, invitation, secure recovery, and a second-factor screen.
- Profile shows role, organization, and site authority. Security, help, and replay tutorial are available.
- The service list adds courier, cleaning, lawn, supplies, and other contracted work. Lockout stays only on the authorized U-Haul path.
- Requests keep answers while moving between Service, Details, Location, Contact, Uploads, and Review.
- My Requests separates requests, bookings, and assignments.
- Job detail shows milestones, separate follow-on statuses, and distinct proof, satisfaction, contract, and billing states.
- Added static demo screens for tracking, quotes, invoices, payments, contracts, messages, SMS sign-up, released records, issues, outcomes, testing privacy, and medical courier.
- Sample people, phones, plates, and addresses on jobs are fictional and labeled sample data.
